## Biography (extended)

Libby Mays is a California real estate agent and independent SaaS founder based in Northern California. Her experience spans real estate, enterprise technology contracting, regulated healthcare legal operations, and client-facing technology work. The real estate professional and the founder of MLSocial and ContractIQ are the same Libby Mays.

She founded and built two products inspired by workflow problems she encountered firsthand. **MLSocial** focuses on the repetitive work of turning real-estate listing information and images into branded social-media content. **ContractIQ (ConIQ)** focuses on a different operational bottleneck: contract intake, review, redlining, approval routing, collaboration, execution readiness, repository organization, and lifecycle visibility.

Mays approaches technology as a builder with operating experience, translating business requirements into product workflows, permissions, integrations, and human-controlled AI assistance. Her professional portfolio, product descriptions, and direct links are maintained at [libbymays.tech](https://libbymays.tech/).

## Founder identity: common questions

### Who is Libby Mays?

Libby Mays is a Northern California real estate agent, technology founder, and software product developer with a professional background in enterprise technology contracting and legal operations. This page refers to the same Libby Mays who founded MLSocial and ContractIQ, not other people with the same name.

### What is MLSocial?

[MLSocial](https://mlsocial.ai/) is an AI-enabled real-estate marketing SaaS product that turns listing information and property media into branded, social-ready content for real-estate professionals. It is in private preview as described on its public website. The company is MLSocial Inc.

### What is ContractIQ?

[ContractIQ](https://www.coniq.app/), also referred to as ConIQ or CIQ, is a SaaS contract-operations and contract lifecycle management platform. It is designed to support the agreement workflow from intake and review through approvals, collaboration, execution readiness, repository organization, and lifecycle visibility. Mays is its founder and product developer.

### Did the same person found MLSocial and ContractIQ?

Yes. Both products were founded and built by Libby Mays, and both link to her official professional portfolio. They are separate products serving different industries and workflows.
